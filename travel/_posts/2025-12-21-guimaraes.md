Guimarães is called the birthplace of Portugal. I visited it to build a more personal connection to the country. I imagine right here, in this gothic canopy, the country was born. I think it cried a lot as a child.
<center>
    <img src="/assets/img/travel/2025/pt/guimaraes/1.jpg" />
    <p class="image-label">Oliveira square</p>
</center>

The old town is beautifully preserved and has a medieval atmosphere. Turns out, Portugal was always gloomy.

    <img src="/assets/img/travel/2025/pt/guimaraes/2.jpg" />
    <p class="image-label">Old town</p>
</center>
<center>

</center>

The castle somehow looks out of place: a brutal building on a green lawn. Then again, it _is_ supposed to be intimidating. But there's just something too perfect about it, that makes it seem unreal. I wish I had seen it at night too.
<center>
    <img src="/assets/img/travel/2025/pt/guimaraes/5.jpg" />
    <p class="image-label">Guimarães Castle</p>
</center>

It seems appropriate that Portugal grew up to build solemn monuments.
