I arrived in Orsomarso for a day trip. It was just me and an older lady on the morning bus. The village had a wintery feel: it was quiet, chilly and smelled of chimney smoke. It's almost 9AM. What a convenient place to put a clock, by the way.

Orsomarso has a long history with Greek heritage: the valley once had Eastern Orthodox monasteries and hermits' caves (see [Mercurion](https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mercurion){:target="_blank"}). In one of the nearby caves, above the village, [Saint Nilus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nilus_the_Younger){:target="_blank"} lived as a hermit.

</center>
