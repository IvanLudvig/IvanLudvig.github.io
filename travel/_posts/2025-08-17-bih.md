After a short wait by the side of the road next to a bullet-riddled sign outside of [Herceg Novi](/travel/2025/montenegro#Herceg-Novi), I got picked up. Once across the border, I said "beautiful Bosnia", which turned out to be a mistake. The driver corrected me: this was actually Herzegovina, and began a long story about this complicated country and its divisions.

And so began my journey in Bosnia and, of course, Herzegovina.

{%- assign verbose-name = 'Banja Luka' -%}

I've got nothing to show from Banja Luka. I was surprised how few landmarks there are in the city, given it's the second biggest by population in the country and the capital of Republika Srpska. It's just a city. Why did I come here? I had a flight.

<center>
    <img src="/assets/img/travel/2025/ba/banja-luka/1.jpg" />
    <p class="image-label">Banja Luka airport</p>
</center>

I ended up visiting four cities: Trebinje, Mostar, Sarajevo and Banja Luka. Which means I've visited all geographical and political combinations (Bosnia/Herzegovina x Federation/Srpska). Nice!
